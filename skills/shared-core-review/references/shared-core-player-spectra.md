# Player spectra

Part of the shared-core-review skill (spectra are computed at every ingest; the reviewer override is a Full-review step). Read when a skill points here.

### Player spectra (from the player)
Describe player traits as **spectra between two opposite ends**, scored 1-5 (3 = in between), alongside the archetype in every player read. `tools\spectra.py` computes them from the captures (with `tools\behavior.py` / `beh_cache.json`); cut points are the 20/40/60/80th percentiles of every player-set in sf6.db **except the configured player's**, each other player weighted equally (min(1, games/10), so players with under 10 games count less); `tools\spectra_calibrate.py DB` recalibrates them, stores them in sf6.db (settings `spectra_cuts`) and re-scores every set. So a score compares the player with everyone else. **Personal baseline:** `tools\player_baseline.py` (run at every ingest) compares the configured player's set with the median of their own sets in the previous 7 days (table `player_baseline`: value, baseline, delta, and the change on the 1-5 scale for each spectrum). Each score comes with its evidence line.
- **Temperament (defense):** Impulsive ↔ Mindful. Share of after-block / wake-up choices made on reversal timing (an attack, special, super, throw, jump or parry within 2 frames).
- **Temperament (offense):** Impulsive ↔ Mindful. After their attack is blocked, how often they press again (attack, jump, Drive Rush) within 3 frames of being free.
- **Adaptability:** Inelastic ↔ Elastic (redefined 2026-11-27). Did the opponent's best weapon keep working? The move that did the most damage to the player in the first half of the set's rounds (2,500+), and its damage per round in the last half as a share of the first half: a low share (the weapon stopped working) is elastic, the same or more is inelastic. Needs 4+ rounds. Winners now score more elastic than losers in about 2 sets in 3 where they differ (the old reuse-of-punished-moves measure pointed the other way and sat at 3 in 38% of sets).
- **Risk tolerance:** Low ↔ High. Risky options per round (wake-up reversals, Drive Impacts, Drive Reversals, raw Drive Rushes at half weight, supers, forward jumps).
- **Raw Drive Rush in risk/reward (from the player):** treat it as fairly low risk: it costs 1 Drive bar, plus a clean or counter hit if it's checked, and it's a generally safe, easy way to start pressure. It only becomes a problem when the opponent keeps checking it and the player doesn't adjust; flag that as a leak, not the Drive Rush itself. Don't weigh character-specific Drive Rush strength for now.
- **Initiative:** Reactive ↔ Proactive. Forward share of neutral movement, averaged with the share of neutral exchanges they start.
- **Composure:** Steady ↔ Tilts. Action rate after taking a 2,000+ combo or losing a round, against the rest of the time.
- **Variety:** Scripted ↔ Mixed. Spread of their after-block and wake-up choices.
- **Cash-in:** Speculative ↔ Cashes in (added 2026-11-27). Share of the Drive and super bars they spent that went into combos that landed (each hit row's spend inside the combo), out of all they spent (rushes, parries, OD moves and cancels included). Needs 3+ bars spent. Pair it with Resource use (how much): spendthrift and cashing in is converting, spendthrift and speculative is the leak. The set winner scores higher in about 2 sets in 3.
- **Space:** Gives ground ↔ Takes ground (added 2026-11-27). Share of live time spent cornering the opponent minus share spent cornered (`corner_time`, from `tools\corner_time.py`: within 1.5 of the wall with the opponent on the inner side). Labels: Gives ground, Leans to giving ground, Even, Leans to taking ground, Takes ground. The set winner scores higher in about 3 of 4 sets, partly because winning pushes the opponent back; a zoner parked in the corner by choice reads as giving ground. Calibrate only this axis with `spectra_calibrate.py DB --axes space`.
- **Resource use:** Conservative ↔ Spendthrift. Drive bars + half a point per super bar + 1 per burnout, per round. Pair it with Drive efficiency: spendthrift and efficient is cashing in, spendthrift and wasteful is a leak.
- **Generated archetypes (Quick reviews, recalibrated 2026-11-27):** `toolsrchetype_calibrate.py DB` sets the cut points from the pool without the configured player (settings `archetype_cuts`) and rewrites every Quick set's generated read; Full / Tournament reads are hand-written and kept. Per round: pressing share after blocking / on wake-up, Drive Rush pressure (0.5 x raw + pressure cancels), forward jumps minus the character's pool average, and counter hits + punish counters. Aggression = the mean pool percentile of the first three. **Aggressive** = aggression in the pool's top 30%; **Fundamentalist** = counter hits / punish counters in the top 30% with aggression below the 60th percentile; **Defensive** = aggression in the bottom 30%; **Balanced** = the rest. The read's evidence line gives both percentiles. When writing a Full review's archetype by hand, use the same meanings. **Lean** (2026-11-27): up to two tags, no numbers, for where the player is in the pool's top or bottom 20%: Drive Rush pressure / rarely Drive Rushes, presses on defense / holds block, jumps a lot for the character / stays grounded, counter-pokes and punishes, throw-heavy, anti-airs a lot, Drive Parry-heavy. Hand-written leans should reuse these words where they fit, so the Archetypes page can count them together.
- **Reviewer's call:** in a Full review, when my read of the video disagrees with a computed score, my call wins: set `spectra_override` `{key: [score, reason]}` in that player's archetypes row, keyed by the spectrum's internal key, not its name: `temperament`, `temperament_off`, `elasticity` (Adaptability), `risk`, `initiative`, `composure`, `variety`, `resources`, `space`, `cashin` (an axis name like "Adaptability" is silently ignored), and the card shows "Reviewer's call (computed X/5)". Quick reviews use the computed scores.
- When a new behaviour-based spectrum looks useful, suggest it to the player rather than adding it silently.
